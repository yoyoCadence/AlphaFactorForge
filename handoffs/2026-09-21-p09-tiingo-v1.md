## Remaining external acceptance and limitations

User must register Tiingo and store the token in Windows Credential Manager:
generic target `com.alphafactorforge.desktop/tiingo`, username `tiingo`.
Do not paste tokens into chat or config. Instructions are in
[`../docs/market-source-tiingo-v1.md`](../docs/market-source-tiingo-v1.md).

After configuration, run the same small range in an isolated workspace and
record per-symbol metadata, actual entitlement, raw coverage and company-event
availability. 403 or absent payment data is an expected honest degraded
outcome, not permission to upgrade/buy a plan. The official distribution docs
describe differing availability; account response is the evidence.
Do not set `corporateActionsConfirmed` or confirm costs merely to make output OK.
Retain original responses locally; do not commit licensed account datasets.

Until then P09's authenticated acceptance stays **In Progress** on `tasks.md`.
All code/local work is Done, but fixtures and missing-token smoke cannot prove
real free-account success. No claims of full history, automatic research,
forward availability, strategy qualification or paper readiness.
The first release is bounded historical daily data, at most 366 calendar days
and 15 HTTP requests. Source settings are operator-maintained; calendar ranges
are not extrapolated, and old registry assumptions are not silently removed.
