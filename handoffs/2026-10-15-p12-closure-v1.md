## Limits retained

- P12e supports exactly {256, 512, 1024} bars × coefficients {0, 0.3},
  the declared `ar1-uniform-sum` generator/family, B=799 and frozen S2-R3.
  This does not establish calibration on arbitrary markets, lengths,
  distributions or family shapes. The original v1 6.45% failure remains.
  No full final-seed run is repeated for this audit.
- The 744-bar native campaign proves admission/exploration and persistence;
  it gives no confirmation PASS, profitability claim or extension of that
  calibrated set. Its candidate did not pass the exploration Gate.
- P13 still owns frozen confirmation/selection, atomic alpha reservation,
  synchronized final ledger fence, one-time Validation/Test exposure,
  overlap/import/restore handling, crash recovery and information boundaries.
- The registry does not defend against deliberate deletion of all machine
  evidence; the accepted contract refuses to preserve qualification in that
  situation. It does not promise tamper-proof external attestation.
- O1 Results Explorer acceptance, O2 authenticated Tiingo acceptance and
  O4 shared-directory/ACL decisions remain separate. The OS metrics-window
  close/reopen and release error-dialog OK → exit 1 checks also remain open.

## P13 order

Start with a bounded pure **P13a reservation-history validation** slice,
implementing the already adopted alpha-allocation §9.1 decisions: bind records
to family/declaration, validate identity and confirmation number, deduplicate
exact replay, preserve conflicting evidence and stop on two independent
reservations of the same number. Only a validated contiguous history may be
projected to the existing amount-only allocator. This does not reserve alpha
or expose data, so it does not depend on deciding whether confirmation may
run outside P12e's tested set.

Subsequent slices need contracts before implementation: external registry
persistence/binding/transfer; synchronized reservation and immutable batch;
exposure identity and overlap union; recovery and execution; typed commands
and native UI acceptance. The unsupported-configuration/calibration policy
in recalibration-plan §11 remains an explicit P13 design decision, with no
automatic permission to claim calibrated real-market confirmation.
